## 3. Current Goal
Build the Complete Purchase Workflow and connect Purchase, Supplier, Inventory, Warehouse, Goods Receipt, Payment, Accounting and Reports.

## 4. Agreed Purchase Workflow

STEP 1 — Create Purchase Draft
- Select Supplier.
- Select Products.
- Enter quantities, purchase rates, tax and totals.
- Draft can be edited or deleted according to permissions.

STEP 2 — Approve / Place Purchase Order
- Generate a unique Purchase Order number.
- Change status from Draft to Ordered.
- Send the Purchase Order to the Supplier by email.
- Record approver and approval time.
- Record email status and allow retry if email fails.
- Do not increase actual stock at this stage.

STEP 3 — Inventory Pending/Inward Orders
- Ordered quantities should appear as pending incoming stock.
- Link the order to the appropriate warehouse where applicable.
- Show ordered, received and pending quantities.
- Pending incoming quantity is NOT available stock.

STEP 4 — Goods Receipt
- Receive goods against the existing Purchase Order.
- Record actual received quantity and damaged quantity where applicable.
- Support partial receipts.
- Prevent receiving more than the allowed quantity unless explicitly authorized.
- Update Purchase status to Partially Received or Received based on received quantities.

STEP 5 — Automatic Stock-In
- After Goods Receipt is confirmed, automatically update actual warehouse stock.
- Create stock movement/history records.
- Prevent duplicate receipt processing and duplicate stock updates.
- Never increase stock merely because a Purchase Order was approved.

STEP 6 — Warehouse / Stock Transfer
- Allow authorized users to transfer stock between warehouses.
- Reduce stock at the source and increase stock at the destination.
- Record transfer history.
- Do not treat a warehouse transfer as a new purchase.

STEP 7 — Supplier Payment
- Track purchase payable, paid amount and outstanding balance.
- Support partial and full payments.
- Track payment dates, methods and transaction history.
- Show Unpaid, Partially Paid and Paid statuses as appropriate.

STEP 8 — Accounting
- Record purchase/inventory value, supplier payable, payment entries and applicable GST/tax according to accounting rules.
- Prevent duplicate accounting entries.
- Do not create accounting entries blindly without checking existing accounting architecture.

STEP 9 — Reports
- Purchase history and supplier-wise purchase reports.
- Ordered and pending incoming stock.
- Partial and complete goods receipts.
- Warehouse stock and stock movement.
- Supplier outstanding balances and payments.
- Purchase costs and applicable tax reports.

## 5. Purchase Status and Action Rules
- Draft: View, Edit, Approve and Delete according to permissions.
- Ordered: View, History and Receive Goods; do not show normal Edit, Approve or Delete actions.
- Partially Received: View, History and Receive Remaining Goods.
- Received: View and History.
- Cancelled: View and History.

- Cancellation after ordering must use a separate audited cancellation workflow and supplier notification.
- Do not silently change an already ordered purchase back to Draft.
- Retry order email only when appropriate.
- Backend must enforce permissions and status transitions; hiding frontend buttons alone is insufficient.

## 6. Stock-In Sources
- Supplier purchase: Goods Receipt against an existing Purchase Order.
- Opening stock: separate opening-stock workflow.
- Customer returns: return workflow.
- Manufacturing output: production workflow.
- Warehouse transfer: transfer workflow, not a new purchase.

## 7. Company Isolation and Permissions
- All company data must remain isolated by companyId.
- Validate authorization and company ownership on the backend.
- Reuse the existing MineERP permission architecture.
- Do not introduce new models or restructure authentication without first reviewing existing code.
